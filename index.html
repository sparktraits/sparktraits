About Spark Traits

Most tests give you a label. We give you a shape.

Spark Traits started with a simple frustration. Most personality tests either put you in a box or charge you to read about yourself alone. We built the one we wanted: free, honest about the science, and useful on Monday morning.

Built on real science

The Big Five is the most researched model of personality. Our statements come from the public-domain IPIP item set, and we tell you exactly what your scores mean and what they don't.

Scales, not boxes

You get a score from 0 to 100 on each trait, so two "introverts" no longer look the same. Every end of every scale has real strengths.

Results you can act on

Every report ends with your growth edge, three habits to try this week, and an offer to talk it through with a person.
